## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "Haute Tech Minimalism"—the intersection of classic European haute couture editorial sensibility and rigorous, state-of-the-art computational utility. Designed for discerning digital capsule wardrobe curators and fashion-forward tastemakers, the platform must feel deliberate, intellectual, and effortlessly poised. 

The aesthetic marries warm, atmospheric minimalism with precision utility:
- **Atmospheric Neutrality:** Dominated by natural, unbleached textile tones rather than sterile digital whites, giving every garment image breathing room.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Crisp, intentional typographics that shift between high-fashion editorial titling and technical, monoline data labels.
- **Subtle Tactility:** Razor-thin structural borders and translucent glassmorphic surfaces that mirror high-end atelier vitrines and lookbooks.
- **Intelligent Restraint:** Restrained bursts of soft Parisian blue and terracotta indicate AI-driven wardrobe intelligence, cost-per-wear (CPW) metrics, and system feedback without cluttering the aesthetic harmony.

## Colors

The palette references archival atelier materials, tactile papery tones, and muted architectural pigments.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces (`#F0F0EE`):** Warm unbleached off-white/cream serving as the default background, reducing ocular fatigue and grounding digital imagery in an organic physical register.
- **Structural Slate (`#3B4D61`):** Serves as secondary anchoring for structured cards, deep contrast modules, and primary UI control surfaces.
- **Functional Accent (`#486E93`):** Muted Parisian blue utilized for AI recommendations, state changes, focus rings, interactive glyphs, and primary links.
- **Metric Highlight (`#D49160`):** Warm terracotta reserved exclusively for high-value intelligence: Cost-Per-Wear (CPW) insights, capsule optimization badges, status highlights, and alert micro-indicators.
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - Absolute Crisp Black (`#161616`) for display headlines, body copy, and ultra-high contrast emphasis.
  - Muted Neutral Gray (`#818085`) for secondary metadata, subtext, empty state copy, and subdued icons.
  - Warm Divider Slate (`#AFAEAC`) and Hairline Border (`#E2E1DF`) for spatial division without visual fragmentation.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic couture mastheads and contemporary digital ergonomics.

- **Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for high-level curation banners, wardrobe collection titles, modal headings, and seasonal narratives. Should be set at medium to regular weights to maintain delicate serifs and crisp hairlines.
- **System Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and highly legible across dense garment spec sheets, analytics charts, and utility actions.
- **Typographic Rules:**
  - Micro-labels, tags, and category chips must use uppercase treatment in `label-sm` with letter spacing widened to `+0.08em` for premium utilitarian rhythm.
  - Numbers and financial/analytical metrics (e.g., CPW, wardrobe balance) should leverage tabular lining figures in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a strict 8pt spatial grid system (0.5rem increments), maintaining uniform vertical and horizontal cadence.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch zones strictly enforce a `48x48dp` hit area, particularly inside the primary bottom navigation and thumb-accessible quick-action drawers.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width bounded at `1440px`, centered, with `4rem` (64px) margins and `2rem` (32px) gutters.
- **Ergonomics:** Critical capsule controls (outfit swiping, item sorting, filter triggers) are pinned to bottom anchor bars on mobile viewport frames to respect lower one-third thumb-zone mechanics.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through subtle tonal gradation and atelier-inspired frosted glass rather than heavy dropped shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F0F0EE` with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Surface background of solid `#FFFFFF` or translucent `#F0F0EE` supported by a 1px perimeter outline in `#E2E1DF`. Shadow is non-existent or minimal: `0 1px 2px rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Glassmorphic Floating Panels & Drawers):** `rgba(240, 240, 238, 0.72)` background with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`, bounded by a hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(59, 77, 97, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Deep ambient diffusion tinted by slate: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(59, 77, 97, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances architectural precision with soft touch interactions:
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive elements (buttons, input boxes, structural cards) use 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding to evoke crisp stationery and clean lookbook borders.
- **Cards & Sheets (`rounded-lg`):** Wardrobe item cards and bottom sheets step up to 8px (`0.5rem`) for subtle softness.
- **Pill Exception:** Status indicators, AI recommendation chips, CPW tags, and floating circular action triggers break the system intentionally using full pill radii (`9999px`) to distinguish meta-attributes and badges from functional content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#161616` background, `#F0F0EE` text, 4px radius, minimum 48px height. On hover: background `#3B4D61`. Focus ring: 2px offset `#486E93`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** 1px border `#AFAEAC`, transparent background, `#161616` text. Hover transitions to `#F0F0EE` background tint with border `#161616`.
- **AI Trigger:** Muted Parisian blue `#486E93` solid fill with `#FFFFFF` text and subtle pill shape when acting as an inline recommendation pill.

### Garment Cards
- Transparent or crisp `#FFFFFF` display backing with 1px border `#E2E1DF`.
- Image presentation maintains a strict 3:4 aspect ratio with subtle bottom metadata: Item name (`title-md`), Brand/Category (`label-sm` in `#818085`), and CPW Indicator tag docked at top-right.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Tags:** Height 24px, 9999px radius, padding `0 10px`.
- **Metric Highlight Chip (CPW):** Background `rgba(212, 145, 96, 0.12)`, text `#D49160`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 145, 96, 0.3)`.
- **Filter Chip:** Neutral `#F0F0EE` base, 1px border `#E2E1DF`, text `#161616`. Selected state: `#3B4D61` background, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Clean underline or 1px boxed outline `#AFAEAC` with background `#FFFFFF`. Height 48px for ergonomic typing.
- Active focus state sharpens the outline to 1px `#161616` with an accent underline `#486E93`. Placeholder text in `#818085`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Minimum 48x48dp click bounds enclosing a 18x18px visible target. Checkboxes use 2px radius; radios are circular.
- Selected state fills with `#161616` or `#486E93` (for automated AI styling selections) displaying crisp white check glyphs.

### Metric Widgets (Cost-Per-Wear & Wardrobe Analytics)
- Structured panels backed with `#FFFFFF` or glassmorphic blur.
- Numerical value rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 24px semi-bold (`headline-md`) with small terracotta (`#D49160`) delta indicators to highlight high-rotation vs. stagnant investment items.
